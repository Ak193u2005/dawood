About

I'm a B.Tech Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning student at S.R.M Institute of Science and Technology, graduating in 2027. Somewhere along the way, the part of the course I looked forward to most wasn't the model training — it was the step before it: cleaning, structuring, and making sense of the data in the first place.

That's pulled me toward data analytics and business intelligence. I like the process of taking a spreadsheet nobody wants to look at and turning it into a dashboard that answers a real question — which region is underperforming, which customers are about to churn, why attrition is climbing in one department.

Right now I'm building on that foundation by learning cloud computing and machine learning, so I can eventually work with data at a larger scale and add a predictive layer to the analysis I already do. My immediate goal is an internship where I can do this kind of work for real.
